# Research basis

Research basis: PRISMA 2020 encourages transparent reporting of review methods and results (Page et al., 2021, https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.n71). Cochrane Handbook guidance emphasizes careful, piloted data collection and checking extracted data (https://training.cochrane.org/handbook/current). This tool models an auditable extraction record with source location and verbatim supporting quote, but it does not extract facts automatically. Structured formats improve portability; they do not prove accurate interpretation.

## Limits

The program validates presence and structure only. It does not interpret articles, extract or verify facts, assess study quality, or establish that a quote supports an entered value. Researchers must check every extraction against the cited source and apply review-specific data handling rules.
